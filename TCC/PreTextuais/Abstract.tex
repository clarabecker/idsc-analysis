	\chapter*{Abstract}
	\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
	
	The quality of basic education is frequently assessed in isolation, without considering the socioeconomic and environmental factors that impact school performance outside the classroom. In this context, the indicators of the Sustainable Development Cities Index (IDSC) offer a multidimensional perspective that can be explored to understand educational performance at the municipal level. This work proposes to analyze the performance of decision tree-based predictive models in the classification task of the performance bands of the Basic Education Development Index (IDEB) in the municipalities of the state of Santa Catarina, using IDSC data. The methodology comprises stages of exploratory data analysis, preprocessing with preventive removal of data leakage, feature selection by correlation filtering and removal of indicators related to education, as well as cross-validation for hyperparameter tuning. The algorithms \textit{Random Forest}, \textit{XGBoost}, \textit{LightGBM}, and \textit{CatBoost} were compared, evaluated separately for the Early Years and Final Years of elementary school, through accuracy, precision, \textit{recall}, and F1-\textit{score}. The feature importance analysis showed that public health indicators (SDG 3) concentrate the largest share of importance in separating the IDEB bands in the final years. The results indicate an association between the socioeconomic context of Santa Catarina municipalities and the IDEB performance bands, providing quantitative and interpretable grounds for the planning and formulation of integrated public policies in educational management.
	
	\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Basic Education Development Index; Machine Learning; Sustainable Development Goals; Model Interpretability; Educational Public Policies.
